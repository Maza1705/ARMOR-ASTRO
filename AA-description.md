### **AA Training Program**

The AA Training Program is an accelerated system designed to unlock the full human potential of its students.

The core enforced mindset is **mindfulness**. When a soldier notices loss of focus, they acknowledge it without judgment and immediately return attention to the task. Study materials are deliberately engaged across varied emotional states to ensure robustness of learning.

---

### **Daily Activity Rules**

- Showers, including dressing, are limited to **8 minutes**
- Meals are limited to **40 minutes**
- **1 hour daily** is mandatory for news consumption (written and audiovisual)
- Rest periods are permitted **only after 3 hours of focused work**

**Exception:** Rest is allowed within a 3-hour block if two materials are studied and their cognitive domains are significantly different.

---

### **Arts (Perception, Expression, and Pattern Mastery)**

**Purpose:** Train aesthetic intelligence as a tool for cognition, influence, and innovation.

- Music composition (complex systems, emotional engineering, time-based patterning)
- Music performance (precision, stress control, embodied cognition)
- Drawing and visual design (spatial intelligence, rapid externalization of thought)
- Symbolism and semiotics (how meaning is encoded visually and culturally)

> Why it matters: The best tacticians, scientists, and manipulators understand patterns, harmony, and emotional resonance.
> 

---

### **Worldology (Global Systems & Power Dynamics)**

**Purpose:** Build an internal simulation of the world as a living, adversarial system.

- World data & systemic mechanics (feedback loops, incentives, failure modes)
- Economics (formal models + shadow economies)
- History (case studies of collapse, dominance, innovation)
- Geopolitics (realpolitik, alliances, proxy conflict)
- Power structures (governments, corporations, intelligence agencies, non-state actors)

> Students should be able to predict second- and third-order consequences of any global action.
> 

---

### **Humanology (Human Systems & Influence Science)**

**Purpose:** Master the human mind individually and collectively.

- Human behavior and decision-making under uncertainty
- Perception, bias, and cognitive vulnerabilities
- Language as a tool for influence, persuasion, and misdirection
- Social dynamics, group psychology, and crowd behavior
- Cultural psychology and memetics

> Graduates should read people the way engineers read machines.
> 

---

### **Engineering (Applied Creation & Systems Design)**

**Purpose:** Teach students to **build, reverse-engineer, and improvise** under constraints.

- Mechanical, electrical, and digital systems
- Design under scarcity and time pressure
- Failure analysis and rapid prototyping
- Human-centered and adversarial design

> If students can’t fix or weaponize a system in the field, they don’t truly understand it.
> 

---

### **STEM (Reality Modeling & Fundamental Truths)**

**Purpose:** Develop rigorous thinking and the ability to model reality mathematically.

- Mathematics (from abstraction to applied modeling)
- Physics (classical → quantum → systems)
- Chemistry and materials science
- Biology (systems biology, neurobiology)
- Computational thinking and simulation

> This is not academic STEM—it is predictive, weaponizable knowledge.
> 

---

### **Combat Training (Embodied Survival & Violence Literacy)**

**Purpose:** Turn the body into a controlled, adaptive instrument.

- MMA (striking, grappling, transitions)
- Biomechanics of combat efficiency
- Conditioning under fatigue, stress, and injury
- Tactical movement and situational dominance

> Emphasis is on efficiency, not sport.
> 

---

### **Cognitive Training (Mental Performance Engineering)**

**Purpose:** Push the limits of information processing and awareness.

- Working memory expansion
- Visual and auditory perception sharpening
- Processing speed and reaction time
- Attention control and cognitive endurance

> This is where students begin to feel “non-normal.”
> 

---

### **Field Training (Operational Competence)**

**Purpose:** Make knowledge actionable in real environments.

- First aid and trauma response
- Navigation (urban, wilderness, denied environments)
- Driving (defensive, evasive, high-performance)
- Environmental scanning and threat detection
- Deception, signaling, and secure communication

> Field training ensures students are never helpless, anywhere.
> 

---

### **Language (Cognitive Multithreading)**

**Purpose:** Expand mental models through linguistic diversity.

- Choose 6 languages spanning:
    - Different language families
    - Different writing systems
    - High-geopolitical value languages
- Emphasis on fluency, accents, and cultural context

> Language is not communication—it’s thinking in multiple realities.
> 

---

### **MetaLearning & Neuroregulation (Self-Optimization Architecture)**

**Purpose:** Enable conscious control over learning, recovery, emotion, and internal state under any condition.

- Sleep cycles and circadian mastery (performance timing, recovery optimization)
- Sleep control (functioning under deprivation, strategic rest)
- Pain perception and control (interpretation, tolerance, signal vs noise)
- Learning methods (rapid skill acquisition, transfer learning, abstraction)
- Emotional regulation (stress, fear, anger, attachment, morale stability)

> The goal is not comfort—it is voluntary control over the internal environment.
>
